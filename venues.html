NAIG 2027 — Venue Plan

Which sport plays where.

Updated 13 October 2026Games: Thu 25 – Sun 28 March 2027Logistics

The two scenarios

They differ in one place only. Everything else is identical.

Scenario 1 — default

  • Men's and women's matches both at Railroad Park, on lit grounds that are already locked.
  • Lake Park's existing pitch carries daytime matches.
  • One site, one setup, one crew. Shortest travel for athletes and volunteers.
  • Trade-off: Lake Park has no lighting, so the schedule has to absorb any delays at Railroad.

Scenario 2 — only if the Texomo contract lands

  • Men's matches move to Texomo in Calera, Oklahoma — five grounds on one site.
  • Women's matches stay at Railroad Park.
  • Lake Park drops to soccer 7v7 backup only.
  • Trade-off: two sites instead of one, so two setups, two crews and two schedules, plus the travel to Calera.
  • Not selectable yet — the Texomo contract has not been received.

What we need from Sports

  • Can the draw run entirely in daylight at Lake Park? If yes, Scenario 1 holds comfortably. If evening slots are needed, Railroad carries more and the schedule tightens.
  • Is splitting men's and women's across two sites acceptable? That is what Scenario 2 means in practice — it affects officials, equipment and how finals are staged.
  • Track and field has no venue. If Sports has a facility in mind, now is the moment.
  • Opening ceremony — the stadium is still to be decided.
