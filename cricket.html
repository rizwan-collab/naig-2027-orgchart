Cricket format

Every option has 44 games. Open games take 3.5 hours and women's 2.5 hours, with 15 minutes between games.

  • Open, 13 teams: 3 pools, 3 pool games each. Then 5 play-ins, 4 quarterfinals (pool winners skip the play-ins), 2 semifinals and the final.
  • Women's, 5 teams: 1 pool, 3 pool games each. Then a play-in (4th v 5th), 2 semifinals and the final.
